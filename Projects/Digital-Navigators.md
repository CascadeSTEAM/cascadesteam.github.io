**FREE** Technology Support is provided by [***Cascade STEAM***](https://cascadesteam.org) volunteer technology students and professionals via our [***Digital Navigators***](https://cascadesteam.org/digital-navigators) program.

Support includes:
* Most technology devices, such as phones, tablets, and computers with Microsoft Windows, Apple MacOS, Linux, iPhone, and Android operating systems
* Web browsing, email, cloud computing, anti-virus, data migration, password management, and other privacy and security concerns
* Software installation, updates, and troubleshooting
* Installation of Linux to provide a FREE and secure replacement operating system for most computers (even those now unsupported by the manufacturer)

Technology education and training programs are also provided upon arrangement.

## Extend the life of older devices

Is your computer no longer supported by the original manufacturer? This is a HUGE privacy and security risk\! We can help install a new, secure, and **FREE** Linux operating system to give a new life to your computer.

* [***All versions of Microsoft Windows prior to Windows 11 are now no longer supported by Microsoft***](https://endoflife.date/windows)
* [***All versions of Apple macOS prior to macOS 14 are now no longer supported by Apple***](https://endoflife.date/macos)
