FREE Community Tech Support is now available from Cascade STEAM via our *Digital Navigators* program. Assistance is offered to the public from technology students and industry professionals through collaboration with social welfare organizations at scheduled onsite events to provide FREE basic technology education and technology support.

To receive support for more complicated or longer time-frame technology issues, please refer to our [*Help Desk*](Help-Desk) Program.

Are you a social welfare organization that would like to arrange collaboration with *Digital Navigators*? Please reach out! We would love to collaborate with you to support your community members.

Contact us at [digital-navigators@cascadesteam.org](mailto:digital-navigators@cascadesteam.org) for questions or comments regarding *Digital Navigators*.
