*Consulting* is now available from Cascade STEAM \- leveraging our team’s extensive combined experience collaborating with employer, nonprofit, education, and local government partners in service of their business, technology, and community engagement goals.

Our **Consulting Engagements** are led by highly experienced business and technology professionals leveraging trusted local service providers when needed, and as appropriate and upon arrangement: distinguished students and accomplished mentors supporting them via [*Service Corps*](Service-Corps) \- our technology training and community service program.

**How It Works**

For small projects we provide a FREE Recommendation as a community service:

We’ll meet for an **Introduction**, do some basic **Discovery** to explore connected topics, discuss your general options, and provide our FREE **Recommendation**.

If we can be of assistance, and upon your interest, we will provide a written **Proposal** to create a **Consulting Engagement** to work with you on your project.

For larger projects:

With your agreement we will launch a **Consulting Engagement** and perform an **Assessment**, which includes an expanded **Discovery**, confirmation of details and priorities in a **Discovery Discussion**, collection of findings in a written **Assessment**, followed by a written **Recommendation** and **Recommendation Discussion**.

If we can be of further assistance, and upon your interest, we will provide a written **Proposal** to continue our **Consulting Engagement**.

**Cost**

Consulting Engagements are varying and negotiable. Costs will vary based on the type of work performed, experience and expertise required, and (optional) approval to include student interns and mentors via *Service Corps* to perform appropriate, supervised work, which is highly discounted. We also provide a 20% discount to fellow non-profit organizations.

**Everyone Wins\!**

Businesses and Organizations receive discounted consulting services from highly experienced professionals. Interns get valuable training and real-world, hands-on experience. Cascade STEAM re-invests in our *Service Corps* program. And we all contribute to *Building a collaborative, supportive, and civic-minded STEAM community for social good.*

Ready to get started? Email us at [consulting@cascadesteam.org](mailto:consulting@cascadesteam.org).
