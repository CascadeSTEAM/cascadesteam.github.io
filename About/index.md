# Mission

Cascade STEAM is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization based in Bellingham, WA dedicated to *Building a collaborative, supportive, and civic-minded STEAM community for social good.*

STEAM is an acronym for the fields of Science, Technology, Engineering, Arts, and Mathematics. STEAM education is a powerful framework to promote curiosity and creativity, encourage a shared learning experience, and build tools to solve important life challenges.

# Code of Conduct

Simplified, our [Code of Conduct](https://cascadesteam.org/conduct) is: Be honest, respectful, and professional. All Cascade STEAM participants agree to abide by this Code of Conduct regarding their behavior. Please review in full and accept these terms to participate in Cascade STEAM activities.

# Asks

Our [Asks](https://cascadesteam.org/asks): Your participation and financial support through [Donation](https://cascadesteam.org/donate) and [Sponsorship](https://cascadesteam.org/sponsorship) will help us in our mission.

# Donate

Cascade STEAM is community supported - we are NOT a member organization with member dues. If you value our work and have the financial means to support us, then please donate! Your donation will help fund our operations and programs. Any donation amount is appreciated. See our [Donate](https://cascadesteam.org/donate)) page for additional details and payment options. Thank you for your support!

# Sponsorship

We welcome and celebrate your financial support as an annual Gold, Silver, or Bronze [Sponsor](https://cascadesteam.org/sponsorship)\! Your financial support will provide continued operation of our organization and programs, including [***Community Groups***](https://cascadesteam.org/community-groups) and [***Community Projects***](https://cascadesteam.org/community-projects), and support new community building and community service programs in development.

# Organization

Please see our [Organization Page](https://cascadesteam.org/organization) to review our mission and structure. Cascade STEAM is made possible through the commitment of volunteers, [Leadership](https://cascadesteam.org/leadership), and open collaboration of [Partners](https://cascadesteam.org/partners). Thank you all for your efforts.
