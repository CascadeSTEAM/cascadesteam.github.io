![[garthjohnson.jpeg|image-right]]I am a DevOps professional and autodidact with a passion for solving issues and sharing my knowledge. Over the years, I have worked for several different organizations, including Microsoft, DARPA, and IBM. I have been instrumental in multiple startups achieving lift-off. I served several years in the US Army as a psychiatric nurse and battle systems strategist. While I still perform selective contract work-for-hire, I am now primarily a full-time instructor at Bellingham Technical College and an activist in the local tech-ed community.

## Role at Cascade STEAM

**Vice President & Treasurer**, Board of Directors

Lead organizer of [Cascade Breach](../../Projects/Breach) — our monthly collaborative CTF program. Brings security governance expertise from the Cloud Security Alliance to Cascade STEAM's program and infrastructure design.

Co-organizer of the [Open Source Community Group](../../Groups/Open-Source). Former Board member of [Bellingham Makerspace](https://bellinghammakerspace.org), where I designed and continue to maintain the network infrastructure for community members.

## Career

43 years of active work in technology:

- **US Army** (1983–1990) — Computer Operations Specialist; missile systems networking and security; psychiatric nurse and EMT, Würzburg, Germany
- **BioCEDE** (1989–2011) — 21 years building software for networks, fundraising, and VR
- **Microsoft** (1997–1999) — demo applications
- **NetYeti Inc.** (2015–2025) — K-12 and collegiate curricula, mentorship
- **Dealer Information Systems** (2016–2022) — DevOps Lead
- **TAGNW** (2019–2023) — Community Organizer
- **Bellingham Makerspace** (2021–2023) — Board of Directors; network infrastructure design
- **Arch Systems** (2022–2024) — Sr. Manager, Cloud Team
- **Bellingham Technical College** (2019–present) — Instructor, Linux, networking, security, programming
