## At Cascade STEAM

**President** — Board of Directors

Lead organizer for [LinuxFest Northwest](../../Projects/LinuxFest-NorthWest). Co-organizer of [Citizen Science](../../Groups/Citizen-Science) and [Data Engineering](../../Groups/Data-Engineering) community groups. Finance and Engagement committee lead.

## Background

22 years spanning enterprise IT management, nonprofit leadership, and workforce development:

- **Dillanos Coffee Roasters** (2004–2008) — Director of IT
- **Ovation Technical Services** (2010–2015) — technical services
- **3D Corporation** (2015–2018) — Business Systems Manager
- **Interconnect Systems** (2018–2019) — Business Development Manager
- **TAGNW** (2019–2023) — Interim Executive Director, then Executive Director; led the organization's transition from a trade association to a 501(c)(3) education nonprofit
- **Computing For All** (2023–2025) — Director of Development; formed the Washington Tech Workforce Coalition; worked on the Washington Jobs Initiative
- **Cascade STEAM** (2023–present) — President
