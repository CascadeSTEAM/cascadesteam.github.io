We welcome and celebrate your financial support as an annual [***Sponsor***](https://cascadesteam.org/sponsorship)\! Your financial support will provide continued operation of our organization and programs, including [***Community Groups***](https://cascadesteam.org/community-groups) and [***Community Projects***](https://cascadesteam.org/community-projects), and support new community building and community service programs in development. Join us in our mission of ***Building a collaborative, supportive, and civic-minded STEAM community for social good***.

# Levels

## Copper: **\$2,500**
* Spirited collaboration with our business, education, local government, and nonprofit [***Partners***](https://cascadesteam.org/partners)  
* Name and logo displayed on our website and in-person events

## Bronze: **\$5,000**
Everything in Copper, plus:
* Exhibition table and Event Sponsorship at our Annual Conference in 2027

## Silver: **\$10,000**

Everything in Bronze, plus:  
* Exclusive Sponsor of one of our **[*Community Groups*](https://cascadesteam.org/community-groups)** or **[*Community Projects*](https://cascadesteam.org/community-projects)**  

## Gold: **\$20,000**

Everything in Silver, plus: 
* A [***Collaborative Internship***](https://cascadesteam.org/collaborative-internship) included or donated to one of our nonprofit [***Partners***](https://cascadesteam.org/partners)  
* A [***Consulting Engagement***](https://cascadesteam.org/consulting) included or donated to one of our nonprofit [***Partners***](https://cascadesteam.org/partners)
