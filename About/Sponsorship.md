We welcome and celebrate your financial support as an annual Gold, Silver, or Bronze [***Sponsor***](https://cascadesteam.org/sponsorship)\! Your financial support will provide continued operation of our organization and programs, including [***Community Groups***](https://cascadesteam.org/community-groups) and [***Community Projects***](https://cascadesteam.org/community-projects), and support new community building and community service programs in development. Join us in our mission of ***Building a collaborative, supportive, and civic-minded STEAM community for social good***.

# Levels

Bronze: **$5,000**

* Spirited collaboration with our business, education, local government, and nonprofit [***Partners***](https://cascadesteam.org/partners)  
* Name and logo displayed on our website and in-person events  
* Exhibition table at our new Annual Conference Event

Silver: **$10,000**

Everything in Bronze, plus:  
* Exclusive Sponsor of one of our **[*Community Groups*](https://cascadesteam.org/community-groups)** or **[*Community Projects*](https://cascadesteam.org/community-projects)**  

Gold: **$20,000**

Everything in Bronze, plus: 
* A [***Collaborative Internship***](https://cascadesteam.org/collaborative-internship) included or donated to one of our nonprofit [***Partners***](https://cascadesteam.org/partners)  
* A [***Consulting Engagement***](https://cascadesteam.org/consulting) included or donated to one of our nonprofit [***Partners***](https://cascadesteam.org/partners)  

# Let’s Collaborate

Please contact us at [***sponsorship@cascadesteam.org***](mailto:sponsorship@cascadesteam.org) regarding [***Sponsorship***](https://cascadesteam.org/sponsorship).
