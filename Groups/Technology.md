Cascade STEAM Technology is a [Community Group](https://cascadesteam.org/community-groups) with a focus on technology and technology-related [Community Projects](https://cascadesteam.org/community-projects). Anyone interested in technology-related technical topics, discussion, socializing, community-building, and collaboration among local organizations is welcome to attend.

As of November 2026 we have combined our separate Artificial Intelligence, Cyber, Data Engineering, and Information Technology meetings into a single Technology event.

## Meetings

We plan to meet monthly at various locations in Bellingham on the second Wednesday of the Month. Meeting details will be available on the [Cascade STEAM Community Hub](http://hub.cascadesteam.org) and the [Cascade STEAM Events Page](http://events.cascadesteam.org).

## Online Discussion

Online discussion, group organization, and community project planning occurs at the [Cascade STEAM Community Hub](http://hub.cascadesteam.org) in the **#technology** channel.

## Contact Us

Please contact [technology@cascadesteam.org](mailto:technology@cascadesteam.org) for community group inquiries.
