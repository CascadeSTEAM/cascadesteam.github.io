Spectrum is a Cascade STEAM [Community Group](https://cascadesteam.org/community-groups) with a focus on all things wireless, including WiFi, Meshtastic and Meshcore, and Amateur Radio.

## **Meetings**

Meeting details will be available on our [Community Hub](http://hub.cascadesteam.org/), [Community Events](/events) page, and our [Events](http://events.cascadesteam.org) platform.

## **Discussion**

Online Discussion occurs on our [Community Hub](http://hub.cascadesteam.org) in the **#spectrum** channel.

## **Contact Us**

Please contact [spectrum@cascadesteam.org](mailto:spectrum@cascadesteam.org) for community group inquiries.
