Data Engineering is a Cascade STEAM [Community Group](index) with a focus on data engineering topics, including artificial intelligence (AI), machine learning (ML), and large language models (LLM), and welcoming participation in [community projects](../Projects/) from a data engineering perspective.

## Meetings

We plan to meet ~monthly on varying days and locations. Meeting details will be available on the [Cascade STEAM Community Hub](http://hub.cascadesteam.org) and the [Cascade STEAM Meetup Page](https://www.meetup.com/cascadesteam).

## Organizers

[Volunteer Organizers](../About/Leadership/) of the Cascade STEAM Data Engineering Community Group are [Eric Veal](https://www.linkedin.com/in/ejveal/) and [Michael Gan](https://www.linkedin.com/in/michaelbgan/). Please contact [data-engineering@cascadesteam.org](mailto:data-engineering@cascadesteam.org) for general inquiries.
