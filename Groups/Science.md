Science is a Cascade STEAM [Community Group](/community-groups) with a focus on science and science-related [Community Projects](https://cascadesteam.org/community-projects). Anyone interested in science-related technical topics, discussion, socializing, community-building, and collaboration is welcome to attend.

## Meetings

We plan to meet monthly at various locations in Bellingham restarting in January 2027.

Meeting details will be available on our [Community Hub](http://hub.cascadesteam.org/), [Community Events](/events) page, and our [Events](http://events.cascadesteam.org) platform.

## Online Discussion

Online discussion occurs on our [Community Hub](http://hub.cascadesteam.org) in the **#science** channel.

## Contact Us

Please contact [science@cascadesteam.org](mailto:science@cascadesteam.org) for community group inquiries.
